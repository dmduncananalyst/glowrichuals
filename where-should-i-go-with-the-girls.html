Where should I go with the girls?

Choose a place with beautiful surroundings, good food, shared activities, and enough free time for everyone to enjoy it differently.

Short answer

Pick the mood first, then the destination.

See the retreats

Agree on the mood

Decide whether the group wants beaches, nightlife, culture, movement, or quiet luxury. A clear mood prevents ten people from planning ten different trips.

Make the logistics easy

Look for comfortable rooms, dependable transportation, and a schedule that does not require one friend to manage every detail.

Leave room for real time together

The best girls trips are not packed from morning to night. Plan a few memorable anchors, then protect space for long meals, wandering, and rest.
