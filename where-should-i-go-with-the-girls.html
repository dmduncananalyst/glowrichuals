Where should I go with the girls?

For a girls trip, choose the destination based on the group mood first. Miami works for nightlife and beach time, Mexico City for food and culture, Palm Springs for a relaxed house weekend, Lisbon for an international city break, and Oahu for beach plus activities.

Miami when the group wants energy

Miami is easy to understand as a girls trip. You can do a beach day, dress up for dinner, go out at night, and still have plenty of brunch and spa options the next day.

The biggest decision is where to stay. South Beach is convenient for nightlife and beach access, while Brickell can feel more polished and city focused.

Mexico City when food is the plan

Mexico City works beautifully for groups that care more about restaurants, design, museums, neighborhoods, and late dinners than lying by a pool all day. Roma Norte and Condesa are popular bases because they are walkable and full of places to eat and drink.

A group can spend the day at a museum or market, book a beautiful dinner, and still have plenty of nightlife without needing a resort schedule.

Palm Springs when everyone needs to breathe

A rental house, a pool, a grocery run, one great dinner, and a few planned activities can be enough. Palm Springs is especially good when the group is tired and wants time together more than a packed itinerary.

It also makes budgeting easier because the group can split the house and decide which meals are worth going out for.

Lisbon when you want the trip to feel special

Lisbon gives you tiled streets, viewpoints, restaurants, wine bars, day trips, and an easy excuse to dress up without needing a beach club every day. A day in Sintra or a coastal side trip can break up the city portion.

For a first international girls trip, it can feel adventurous without requiring a complicated multi city plan.

How to prevent group trip drama

Decide the non negotiables before anyone pays. Talk about room sharing, budget, nightlife, wake up times, and whether the group expects to do everything together.

The best girls trips usually have one or two anchor plans each day and enough empty space for people to split up, nap, shop, or do their own thing without it becoming a problem.
