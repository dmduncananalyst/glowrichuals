How can I stay active while on vacation?

Keep vacation movement simple: walk often, pack one short routine, and let the destination create the rest.

Short answer

Keep vacation movement simple: walk often, pack one short routine, and let the destination create the rest.

See the retreats ↗

Lower the setup

A mat-length space and ten to twenty minutes can be enough. Choose a familiar sequence that does not require equipment or a complicated decision first thing in the morning.

Let travel count

Walking through a new city, swimming, hiking, dancing, and carrying your own bags are all movement. You do not need to reproduce your home schedule for the trip to be active.

Choose a trip built around movement

If you want structure without planning every class yourself, a Pilates retreat can make movement part of the destination. You get the ritual of practice and the freedom to enjoy where you are.

This article is general educational information, not individualized medical advice. If pain or health concerns affect your movement, speak with a qualified healthcare professional.
