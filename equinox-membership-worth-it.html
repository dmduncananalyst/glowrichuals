Is an Equinox membership worth it?

It can be worth it when the location, classes, amenities, and atmosphere make you train more consistently than a lower-priced option would.

Short answer

The value is in how often you use the full experience.

See the retreats

Price is only one part

Compare the monthly cost with the classes, equipment, locker rooms, locations, and time you would otherwise spend paying for separate services.

Convenience changes consistency

A premium membership is easier to justify when the club is near home or work and the schedule fits your real week. A beautiful gym across town can still go unused.

Try the routine before committing

Tour the exact location, review the class calendar, and calculate a realistic cost per visit. The answer depends on your habits, not the brand name alone.
