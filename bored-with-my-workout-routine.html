I’m bored with my workout routine. What should I try?

Change the setting, format, or goal before assuming you need to abandon movement altogether.

Short answer

Change the setting, format, or goal before assuming you need to abandon movement altogether.

See the retreats ↗

Change one variable

Try a new instructor, a different Pilates format, an outdoor walk, dance, swimming, or a strength session. A small change can create fresh attention without erasing the progress you already made.

Give yourself something to anticipate

Book a class with a friend, choose a short challenge, or plan movement around a trip. Motivation often returns when a workout becomes connected to an experience instead of another obligation.

Keep what still works

Boredom is information, not failure. Hold onto the movements that make you feel good and replace the parts you are only doing from habit.

This article is general educational information, not individualized medical advice. If pain or health concerns affect your movement, speak with a qualified healthcare professional.
