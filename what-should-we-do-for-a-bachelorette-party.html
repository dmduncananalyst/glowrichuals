What should we do for a bachelorette party?

A great bachelorette trip usually has one memorable activity, one beautiful dinner, a comfortable place to stay, and enough open time for the group to actually enjoy each other. The trip should feel like the bride, not like a checklist from social media.

Start with the bride, not the trend

Ask what she would actually enjoy. Some brides want a pool house and cocktails. Some want a city with restaurants and dancing. Some would rather do a spa weekend, a winery, or a quiet beach trip.

The easiest way to make the weekend feel personal is to pick the destination and activities around her personality instead of copying a generic itinerary.

Choose one hero activity

Give the trip one thing everyone will remember. That could be a private dinner, a boat day, a dance class, a beach picnic, a cooking class, a show, or a beautiful spa afternoon.

One strong plan is usually better than five average plans. It also keeps the budget from spiraling.

Make the money conversation early

Before booking anything, give the group a realistic range for lodging, flights, food, transportation, and activities. People are much less stressed when they know the total shape of the trip before deposits start flying around.

It is also okay to create optional activities. Not everyone has to spend the same amount to be part of the weekend.

Leave white space in the schedule

Groups move slowly. Hair, makeup, coffee, transportation, and getting everyone out the door takes time. If every hour has a reservation, the weekend starts feeling like work.

A little empty space gives the group time to sleep in, sit by the pool, take photos, get ready, or change plans without throwing off the whole day.

A few destinations that work for different moods

Las Vegas is obvious for shows and nightlife. Palm Springs is great for a house, pool, and relaxed group time. Miami gives you beach plus restaurants. Mexico City is excellent for food and culture. Cabo works when the group wants a resort focused international trip.

Pick the place that makes the bride excited, then build the weekend from there.
