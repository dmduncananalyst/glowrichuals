What should we do for a bachelorette party?

Plan one beautiful shared trip around the bride’s actual taste, then add a few standout moments instead of filling every hour.

Short answer

Make the celebration personal, easy to join, and worth traveling for.

See the retreats

Start with the bride

Choose the energy she wants, whether that is beach time, late dinners, movement, culture, or quiet luxury. The trip should feel like her, not a checklist.

Make the money clear

Share the expected price, payment dates, room arrangements, and major inclusions before anyone commits. Clear planning protects the friendships and the celebration.

Create two unforgettable moments

A sunset dinner, a beautiful class, a private boat ride, or a meaningful toast can carry the trip. You do not need constant activities for the celebration to feel special.
