What events are happening in Las Vegas right now?

On September 19, 2026, Las Vegas has several major events and shows, including Eagles at Sphere, the iHeartRadio Music Festival at T Mobile Arena, Wayne Newton at Flamingo, and multiple dayclub and nightclub DJ events across the Strip.

The biggest events tonight

Visit Las Vegas lists Eagles performing at Sphere on September 19, along with the iHeartRadio Music Festival at T Mobile Arena. Those are the kinds of events that can affect hotel demand, traffic, dinner reservations, and rideshare timing around the Strip.

If either event is the reason you are going, choose your hotel and dinner location around the venue instead of treating Las Vegas like one compact neighborhood.

There is also classic Vegas entertainment

Wayne Newton is listed at Flamingo on September 19 as part of his recurring schedule. That gives you a completely different type of night from a stadium or arena concert and can work well if you want something that feels unmistakably Las Vegas.

Check the venue directly before buying because show schedules can change.

Daylife and nightlife are busy too

The official tourism listings for September 19 include Tiesto at LIV Beach, DJ Pauly D at Marquee Nightclub, Don Diablo at TAO Beach, James Hype at Zouk, and several other club events.

If nightlife matters, book the event first and build dinner around it. Trying to do a long dinner across town right before a ticketed club or concert is how the night gets stressful.

Use one main event as the anchor

Choose the concert, show, game, or club night that matters most, then add one great meal and one daytime activity. That is usually enough to make the trip feel full without spending the entire weekend checking the clock.

A pool day, spa appointment, shopping, or Red Rock drive can balance the high energy part of the weekend.

Check the calendar again before you leave

Las Vegas schedules change constantly. Venues add shows, restaurants create special events, and club lineups can shift. Look at the official venue page again a day or two before the trip and confirm ticket details in the official app or email.

That final check matters more than any old list saved from months ago.
