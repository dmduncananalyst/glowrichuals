Is it okay to be a mom and take a vacation without my kids?

Yes. Taking time away does not make you less devoted to your children, and careful planning can make the separation feel secure for everyone.

Short answer

You are allowed to take a trip that is for you.

See the retreats

Guilt is not a travel requirement

Parents can love their children deeply and still want adult time, rest, friendship, and new experiences. Both things can be true.

Make the plan feel dependable

Choose trusted care, leave clear routines and contacts, and decide how often you want to check in. A thoughtful plan can make it easier to be present on the trip.

Come home with something restored

Time away can give you perspective and energy. The purpose is not to escape your family, it is to return with more of yourself available.
