About the Founder

Glow Richuals was created by Elauq, a Pilates instructor and experience driven wellness educator who believes movement can be more than a workout. After building a full-time career teaching Pilates throughout Orange County, she created Glow Richuals as a way to bring movement, intentional experiences and travel together. Elauq is an intentional living enthusiast & wellness educator who believes in the quiet power of morning rituals. What started as a personal practice became a way to make space each morning for movement, meditation, and intention.
