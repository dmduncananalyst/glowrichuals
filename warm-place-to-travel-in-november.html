I have time to travel in November. Where can I go somewhere warm?

Look for destinations entering their dry or shoulder season, with warm days and enough variety for a full vacation.

Short answer

Fiji, New Zealand’s warmer regions, the Canary Islands, and parts of the Caribbean can offer warmth in November.

See the retreats

Choose weather before a postcard

Average temperatures matter, but rainfall, humidity, and daylight can change the feel of a trip. Check the exact region and travel dates before booking.

Match the destination to your pace

A warm trip can mean quiet beach days, city dinners, outdoor movement, or all three. Decide whether you want a restorative week or a full itinerary.

Plan for the whole journey

Compare flight time, connections, hotel quality, transportation, and the experiences included. The best destination is the one that feels worth the travel once you arrive.
