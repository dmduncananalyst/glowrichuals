FAQ - Greece Retreat

Everything to know before Greece.

Booking

Reserve your place

What does the Greece Retreat cost?+

The Greece Retreat is $4,800 for single occupancy and $4,200 for double occupancy.

How do I reserve my place?+

Choose your Greece Retreat occupancy on the Book a Retreat page, then pay the $1,200 deposit through secure checkout. Payment of the deposit reserves your place.

Is the $1,200 deposit refundable?+

Yes. The $1,200 Greece Retreat deposit is refundable through December 30, 2026. Beginning December 31, 2026, the deposit is nonrefundable.

When is the remaining balance due?+

The remaining balance is $3,600 for single occupancy or $3,000 for double occupancy and must be paid in full by April 30, 2027.

Greece

Before you travel

When is the Greece Retreat?+

The Greece Retreat is scheduled for July 17–24, 2027.

Where can I see the full itinerary?+

Your complete itinerary will be emailed after your reservation is confirmed.

Is transportation included for Greece?+

Transportation from the ferry port to the hotel is included on arrival, and transportation from the hotel back to the ferry port is included on departure. Airfare, the ferry ride, travel insurance, and the hotel’s daily climate fee are not included.

The retreat

While you are with us

Who hosts the retreats?+

The Greece Retreat is hosted by Elauq and Sophia. Hosts for future retreats will be announced with each retreat.

Do I need to be advanced at Pilates?+

No. Retreat movement is designed to be welcoming, with options for different experience levels. Share injuries or movement concerns before booking so the hosts can tell you whether the retreat is a good fit.

Are Morocco, Mexico City, and South of France available now?+

Those destinations are coming soon. Join the waitlist to receive an email when booking opens.
